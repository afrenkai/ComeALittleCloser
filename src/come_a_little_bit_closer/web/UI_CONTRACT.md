# Globe explorer contract

`GET /` renders `index.html` with `request`, `models`, and `initial_search=null`. It performs no retrieval. Startup query, model, origin, and genre are blank. The full station catalog appears on a presentation-oriented globe with no origin marker, ranking drawer, or embedding projection. A blank-globe click selects an origin; a search is submitted only if all required inputs are valid. The exact query label is `query text`.

The form sends `latitude`, `longitude`, `genre`, `query`, `model`, and `limit` to `/explore`. `hx-sync="this:replace"` cancels superseded requests. A monotonically increasing request sequence additionally rejects stale swaps and stale errors. Results are replaced atomically. New successful searches open the current rankings drawer. The form, persistent station player, selected view, and globe camera remain outside or survive the replacement. A nonblank genre filter appears as a visible clearable chip.

The result contains `origin`, `query`, resolved `genre`, `genre_selection` (genre, source, matching raw tags), model metadata, `eligible_stations`, `overlap_at_k`, `geographic_neighbors`, `embedding_neighbors`, `unfiltered_embedding_neighbors`, `metrics`, `message`, and nullable `projection`. Neighbors contain station metadata, distance, nullable similarity, genre tags, and stream URL.

Query genre matching uses the complete recorded MusicBrainz vocabulary with normalized literal matches and longest-name precedence. Both spatial rankings use the same genre-eligible pool; no recognized genre means all stations, explicitly labeled. Multiple genres require a disambiguating tag; contradictory query/tag requests return 400. Detected genres are recomputed per request and never written into the persistent manual filter.

Metrics separate raw model tag precision from spatial ranking: `unfiltered_genre_precision_at_k` is measured on unfiltered model neighbors, while mean geographic/embedding distance, excess mean distance, and overlap use the shared eligible pool. The result drawer shows the genre source, raw genre agreement, extra distance, and station tags. Raw neighbors appear in a collapsible section and remain playable. Empty rankings display no comparison; unavailable metrics remain null. Catalog tags are not audio-verified genre truth.

A projection has `method`, `description`, three `explained_variance_ratio` values, real `x`, `y`, `z` for its `query` and every point, `frame_center` (`x`, `y`, `z`), `frame_radius`, and `clusters`. Each point also has station metadata, `stream_url`, retrieval metrics/ranks, and integer `cluster_id`. Each cluster has `cluster_id`, `label`, and `size`. The frontend does not synthesize a third coordinate. Full-corpus center and radius keep the frame stable across queries and genre filters. Changing models resets the embedding camera.

The embedding view uses a perspective 3D camera, depth-sorted points, PC 1/2/3 orientation triad, and D3 Tableau cluster colors. Point fill denotes the full-dimensional k-means group; rings denote geographic, embedding, or shared neighbors. Cluster labels are metadata-derived descriptions, not ground-truth classes. Method, variance, projection caveat, and legends remain in a closed Info popover. Drag orbits; Shift/right-drag pans; wheel/trackpad/pinch zooms. The globe and embedding views support zoom up to 128×, keyboard controls, explicit zoom/reset buttons, and two-pointer touch pinch. Globe rotation sensitivity follows its projected radius; zoom is anchored under the pointer when geometrically possible.

`GET /api/stations` returns the complete cleaned catalog as `station_id`, `name`, `country_code`, `latitude`, `longitude`, and `stream_url`. The frontend fetches and caches it independently of genre filters. The globe renders all catalog stations; PCA and retrieval use the filtered candidate set. Every station dot and list-name button selects its station and starts user-triggered playback. URLs are parsed and restricted to HTTP(S); station names are escaped or assigned with `textContent`. The native audio player is outside `#results`, persists across searches, and exposes pause, mute, retry, errors, and dismissal. Initial page load never starts audio.

Serve `static/` at `/static`. There is no frontend build. HTMX, D3, and official Natural Earth geometries are vendored with sources, licenses, and checksums under `static/vendor/`. No external scripts or map tiles are fetched at runtime. The globe uses true orthographic clipping and progressive 110m/50m country polygons without resizing country geometries. Its natural palette is schematic rather than a land-cover measurement.
